# MASKS · A NEW REMIX
- MASKS: build your own face mask like a game character. Pick a starting mask, flip its parts, tune colours and effects to the drop, then WEAR it in VISUALS.
- REMIX (BETA): a new look and a new engine. Bass that follows the song's key and chords, vocal chops in the drop, GRIT · WOBBLE · SUB · GLIDE, and BUILD about twice as fast.
- SAMPLE LAYERS: add a folder of your own drum one-shots; they play instead of FoxBox's for the parts they cover.
- Face tracking: it follows your head first, so glasses and headphones stay covered, there's one mask per face, always, and it holds on when you look down at the decks.
- IMPORT FROM REKORDBOX: pick tracks from your rekordbox.xml, with their grids and cues, in REMIX, the Studio or VISUALS.
- Safe mid-set: unplug the projector and the output closes, then reopens when it's back; a song dropped while a track plays waits until it stops.
