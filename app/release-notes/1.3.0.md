- LIVE: mask your voice in real time, with a song deck locked to the beat, push-to-talk, MIDI and set recording. LIVE replaces RECORD.
- Visuals: pick a style for the stage: FOXBOX (voice core, feedback tunnel, point cloud, spectral terrain, scope, datamosh, flow field), MILKDROP (100 presets, auto-cycling on the beat) or SHADERS (16, plus your own .fs files), in five palettes.
- The visuals react to the song as well as your voice: kicks pulse, bars cut, and the drop hits.
- OUTPUT sends the stage fullscreen to a projector or LED wall on a second display.
- Camera clips can use any visual style as the picture.
