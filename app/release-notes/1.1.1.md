- Songs in the Studio: import a track under SOURCE and FoxBox reads its BPM, key and bar 1. Your drop lands on its first big beat drop, and you can drag it to any bar.
- Preview the drop over the song before you export, and match the Studio to the song's tempo and key in one click.
- ALSO BAKE INTO SONG: export writes the song with your drop mixed in, next to the drop itself.
- Filmed takes play back: while a filmed take's drop plays, RECORD shows its film in step, faces hidden.
- Camera clips use the same song as the Studio, placed where you put it.
- Camera clips carry a small FoxBox fox in the corner (turn it off in SETTINGS).
- Setup doesn't download models this Mac already has: when they're all here, FoxBox skips Setup and opens straight into the Studio.
