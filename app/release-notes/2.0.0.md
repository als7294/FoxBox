# SAUCE · REMIX
- FoxBox 2.0: an instrument for your hands. Curve them into a C, turned a little toward the camera, and strings of light stretch between your fingertips.
- SAUCE: make sounds, no song needed. Raise or lower a hand to pick a note and curl a finger to pluck it. Two hands play two notes.
- Shapes: a fist drives, a pinch wobbles, peace echoes, horns open up SPACE. Point up or down for the octave, twist to bend, shake for vibrato.
- Seven sounds: 808, REESE, GROWL, WOBBLE, PLUCK, LEAD and KEYS, in any key.
- Melodies: pick one and pluck to play it in your own rhythm, or let it guide your notes. 🎲 rolls a new one.
- REMIX: hold a hand shape and its beat FX plays, locked to the beat. Fold a finger to cut its band. ▶ starts just before the drop.
- Frames: make a frame and the last 4 beats loop inside it. Pinch and hold for a moment to change the effect; turn both hands over for its flip side.
- Library wheel: hold the shaka 🤙 or press L. Your right index spins the sleeves; close your left hand to preview, hold to load. Reads a folder, Rekordbox XML or Serato.
- Board: knobs and faders for the sound or the song. VISUALS adds Bars, Halo, Silk, Grain and Scope.
- Make it yours: Edit shapes in Hands or Beat FX; pluck sensitivity and a left-handed layout in Settings.
- Record: WAV takes in SAUCE, video in REMIX. Drag them into Ableton, Rekordbox or Finder.
- GUIDE: a short tour with your own camera. Press G to replay it.
- Where's the rest?: STUDIO, VISUALS, MASKS and the voice mask live on in FoxBox 1.5.5 on GitHub. Your files are untouched.
