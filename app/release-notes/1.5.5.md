# STRINGS
- STRINGS: remix the drop with your hands. Hold a hand shape and its beat FX plays: a fist tears out, peace chops a riddim, a pinch wobbles, an open palm growls, horns go halftime.
- Point down for a sub drop. Point up for a build roll, then let go to drop. Raise a hand for more depth.
- Play the strings too: stretch them for tension (slack builds dark, taut opens up), tilt them to slide the 808, shake them for vibrato.
- Fold a finger to cut its band: thumb SUB, index LOW, middle MID, ring HIGH-MID, pinky HIGH.
- Frame your hands for the GLASS: a pane onto another world, with its own sound.
- One press of ▶ plays any track, starting just before the drop. Load a track right on STRINGS, or try the TEST BEAT.
- Cleaner stems: less vocal bleed, and the 808 stays with the bass. The stem splitter's weights are Meta's, for scientific use only; the download says so.
- Tracking no longer freezes when the room goes dark (club lights down); face hiding keeps following you.
- FACE HIDING on STRINGS hides your face in one click; RECORD and SEND TO OUTPUT ask first while it shows.
