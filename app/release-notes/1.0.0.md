- Type a line or record your voice: FoxBox masks it with seven presets and a full FX rack, locked to your BPM and bars.
- Endings are never cut: a render grows to fit its tail, and END lands the last word on a beat or a bar.
- The voice core moves with each preset, and a metronome click (M) plays along while you preview.
- Export AIFF or WAV with Rekordbox playlists, or drag the cartridge straight into your DJ software.
- First-run Setup installs the voices with live progress; the persona designer and recording transcripts are optional.
- Updates download and install from inside the app.
