# Play it your way
- LOOP: Melody mode. Pick a melody and it loops on the beat. Right hand up for brighter, left for higher, a fist drops it. S switches PLUCK · FLOW · LOOP.
- The loop strip: your melody's notes under the stage. Hover to drag them; whatever you do stays in key.
- Frames: hold a frame and the last 4 beats loop. Wider for more, let go to stop. Each new frame is the next effect.
- How you play: CURL plucks with a curled finger, PALM taps the air with an open palm, ONE HAND plays everything with either hand. No style is better; switch any time in Settings → Playing.
- Easier plucks: a smaller fold plays a note. Too easy? Settings → Playing → Pluck sensitivity.
- MIDI out: pick the "FoxBox" port in any DAW or synth. Plucks send notes, your hands send CCs.
- FoxBox for Ableton Live: a device that plays your hands into its track, with four macros to map. Settings → MIDI & Ableton.
- Ableton Link: tempo in sync with Live and other Link apps; LOOP, frames and REMIX follow it. Turn it on in Settings → MIDI & Ableton, and in Live.
- MIDI takes: each take also saves a .mid to drag into your DAW.
- Strings of light: SAUCE's and REMIX's strings, redrawn.
- Setup: new installs start with five quick questions: language, face hiding, lead hand, how you play, your music.
- Languages: FoxBox speaks Español, and in beta Português, Français, Deutsch and 日本語. It follows your Mac; change it in Settings → Playing → Language.
