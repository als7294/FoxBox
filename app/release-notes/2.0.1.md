# Play with one hand
- One-handed mode: Settings → Playing → ONE HAND. Either hand plays.
- Hold to switch: hold a shape to turn its effect on or off. Hold one L to make a frame.
- Fits your reach: an adjustable hold time and wobble tolerance, and Calibrate reach sets the notes where your hand is comfortable.
- Keys too: F frames, P changes the frame's style, L opens the library.
- The GUIDE has a one-handed version.
