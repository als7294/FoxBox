## FoxBox 2.1.1: pick your mode

- **Three modes:** SAUCE, MELODY and REMIX. Pick one when you start; switch any time with M.
- **Frames** sit inside your fingers and follow your hands.
- **Windows:** hand tracking now runs on PCs without a working graphics card, more slowly, and tells you if it can't.
- **ONE HAND** no longer asks for your other hand.
- **LOOP:** open hands no longer make a frame by accident.
- **A crisp app icon** in the Dock.

The update is small. Everything new in 2.1 is in [FoxBox 2.1.0](https://github.com/als7294/FoxBox/releases/tag/v2.1.0).

**Mac:** download the DMG (macOS 14+, Apple silicon, a camera). Already on 2.x? FoxBox updates itself in the app.

**Windows beta (SAUCE only, unsigned):** FoxBox-Setup-2.1.1-x64.exe, for Windows 10 and 11 (x64). Windows warns about unsigned installers: choose More info → Run anyway. On the 2.1.0 beta already? FoxBox updates itself when you quit it.

The stem splitter's weights are Meta's (Demucs), licensed for scientific use only; FoxBox says so before the download.
