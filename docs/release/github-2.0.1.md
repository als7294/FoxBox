## FoxBox 2.0.1: play with one hand

FoxBox now plays with one hand. Turn it on in Settings → Playing → One-handed, and either hand plays.

- **Hold to switch:** hold a shape to turn its effect on, and hold it again to turn it off. Hold one L to make a frame, and hold it again to let it go.
- **Fits your reach:** the hold time and the wobble tolerance are adjustable, and Calibrate reach sets the notes between your highest and lowest comfortable heights.
- **Keys too:** F frames, P changes the frame's style, and L opens the library.
- **The GUIDE** has a one-handed version. It opens the first time you turn One-handed on.

A small update; nothing else changes.
