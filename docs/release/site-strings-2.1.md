# /strings/ for 2.1: copy brief (S5)

For the site session, **after the user's 2.1 test and the PM's go**. Nothing ships before that.
The site's rule: a big line plus at most one short line. Fine print stays fine print. Feature names stay in caps.
"Now" means the current foxbox.tech/strings/ copy; "2.1" is the replacement.

## Changed sections

| Section | Now | 2.1 |
|---|---|---|
| Pluck | **Raise a hand to pick a note. Curl a finger to pluck.** / Always in key. The note pops at your fingertip. | Keep as is. CURL is still the default. |
| One hand (replace it) | **Play with one hand.** / Settings → Playing → One-handed. | **Play it your way.** / Curl a finger, tap the air with an open palm, or play it all with one hand. |
| Frames | **Make a frame.** / The last 4 beats loop inside your hands. Pinch to change the effect. Turn your hands over for the flip side. | **Hold a frame.** / The last 4 beats loop while you hold. Wider for more, let go to stop. |
| Make sounds | **Make sounds.** / Seven sounds, two ways to play, and a hand shape for every effect. | **Make sounds.** / Seven sounds, PLUCK, FLOW or LOOP, and a hand shape for every effect. |
| Melody presets (replace it) | **Melody presets.** | **Melodies that loop.** / Pick one and it plays on the beat. Your hands make it brighter, higher or wider, or drop it. |
| The loop strip (new, after Melodies) | (none) | **Edit the loop.** / Drag the notes. Whatever you do stays in key. |
| Record | **Record a take.** / Drag the WAV straight into Ableton. | **Record a take.** / Drag the WAV, or its MIDI, straight into Ableton. |
| MIDI & Ableton (was "Coming in 2.1") | Coming in 2.1: Play any synth with your hands… | **Play any synth.** / Plucks are notes, your hands move knobs. Into any DAW, or the FoxBox device in Ableton Live. |
| Its fine print | The Live device needs Live Suite or Max for Live. | The Live device needs Live Suite, or Standard with Max for Live. Link is off by default: turn it on in FoxBox and in Live. |

Drop every "Coming in 2.1" label.
