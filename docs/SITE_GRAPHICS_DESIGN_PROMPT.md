# foxbox.tech feature graphics: Claude Design prompt

**How to use it:** paste everything below the line into Claude Design. Attach the files listed at the end. Export the result → Handoff to Claude Code, and give the PM the zip.

---

**What this is.** A set of feature graphics for foxbox.tech, the site for two Mac apps from the same maker:
- **FoxBox Strings:** an instrument you play with your hands in front of your webcam.
- **FoxBox Voice (beta):** turns a typed line into a club-ready vocal drop.

Each graphic explains ONE feature at a glance, sits beside a short headline on the page, and loops gently if motion helps.

**The bar.** High-end and minimal: the quality of Apple's or Linear's feature art. One bold idea per graphic, with depth, light and texture, lots of air, and no clutter.
- **Not wanted:** flow-chart lines, dashboards, generic SaaS illustration, stock 3D blobs, or anything that looks AI-default.
- A first in-house attempt was rejected for being too plain and diagrammatic.

**Brand.**
- **Strings, warm night:**
  - background #15100D;
  - ink #F6EFE6;
  - accent ember #F2701F, accent-soft #FFB27A;
  - strings of light that glow like warm filament;
  - glass panes, rgba(28,22,18,.74) with a soft blur and a hairline rgba(255,255,255,.12).
- **Voice, cool night:** deep navy #070B14, snow white, ice blue and pale silver, with one aurora accent running teal to violet.
- **Type:** Geist (400–800) and Geist Mono. Labels are short and sparse, never paragraphs.
- **Motion:** soft and springy. Loops of 3 s or less, with a still frame for reduced motion.
- **No third-party logos or names in the art**, apart from the plain text "Rekordbox XML" and "Serato" on library source chips.

**The set, one graphic each (16:10, plus a 4:5 crop for mobile):**
- **Strings · SAUCE (make sounds):**
  1. **Curve into a C:** two hands in a C, with strings of light stretched between the fingertips.
  2. **Pick and pluck:** raise a hand to choose the note, curl a finger to pluck it, and the note name pops at the fingertip.
  3. **Shapes add effects:** ✊ drive, 🤏 wobble, ✌️ echo, 🤘 space, each as a hand silhouette with its effect visualised.
  4. **Melody presets:** Follow, Fit and 🎲 Roll. Your plucks walk a glowing phrase.
  5. **Frames:** two L-shaped hands hold a glass pane that loops the last 4 beats. Pinch to change the style, and turn your hands over for the flip side.
  6. **Record and drag:** a take becomes a WAV and a .mid that drag into your DAW.
- **Strings · REMIX:**
  7. **Stems split on your Mac:** one song separates into drums, bass, vocals and other.
  8. **Beat FX shapes:** TEAROUT, RIDDIM CHOPS, HALFTIME, BUILD ROLL.
  9. **Fold a finger to cut a band.**
  10. **The library wheel:** a half-dome of record sleeves rises from a waveform. A finger dials, a grab previews, a hold loads.
- **Shared:**
  11. **Everything on your Mac:** nothing leaves the machine.
- **Voice:**
  12. **Type or record.**
  13. **Shape it:** a rack with presets and four macros, DEPTH · GRIT · MACHINE · SPACE.
  14. **Cut to the bar:** `|` lands each part on the beat, and `*word*` throws a word into delay.
  15. **Ready for your DAW:** AIFF/WAV with BPM and key, plus a rekordbox.xml with a hot cue.
- **Coming in 2.1:**
  16. **Play any synth:** hands → the FoxBox device → your synth (match the attached mocks).

**Deliver:** the 16 graphics in one consistent language. Show 3 first (5 · Frames, 10 · The library wheel, 14 · Cut to the bar) in two clearly different directions, so I can pick before the rest.
